Separate exact runtime gates, bounded risk-appropriate semantic review, human decisions, and versioned golden evaluation. Include operator-intent, unchanged-baseline revision, context-fragmentation, lossy-intermediate, repeated/changing-feedback, and high-assurance fixtures. Return only `validation_matrix`.
