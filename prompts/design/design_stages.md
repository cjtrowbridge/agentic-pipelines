Define the smallest ordered graph around the smallest coherent semantic decision, not the smallest individually nameable claims. Declare `semantic_unit`, minimum sufficient context, trusted example roles/selection/budget/omissions, retained-session boundary, review scope, blocking authority, risk tier, stage-split justification, and every lossy intermediate's limited authority. For each transformation or gate also declare property class, mechanism, verdict authority, proof basis, evidence, repair owner, finite progress-tested retry, and next states. Exact code may validate representations but may not launder heuristic, free-text, model-label, typed-proxy, similarity, or lexical evidence into semantic verdicts. Default to retained-session audit; require consequence or measured-failure evidence for independent high-assurance review. Put exact normalization before inference and exact schema/resource/render/promotion gates after it. Return only `stage_design`.
